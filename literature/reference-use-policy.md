# Reference-use policy

A bibliography entry is retained only when it supports a concrete technical, historical, or methodological statement in the manuscript.  Venue-calibration and “papers in this journal often do X” citations are excluded from the publication bibliography.  Identity verification and claim support are separate: DOI/DBLP/publisher metadata establishes which work is cited; the citation-context review establishes why it is cited.  Metadata availability alone is not evidence for the paper's novelty or theorem statements.
